Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If AUK isn't right for you, email skumar5190@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase and you get your money back. You do not need to explain why, and you will not be talked out of it.

1. What is covered

Every AUK licence purchased through our checkout is covered by a full refund for 14 calendar days from the date of purchase. The guarantee applies whether you bought at the launch price or the regular price, and whether you used the app heavily or not at all. We do not require a reason, a survey, or a support call first.

2. Try it before you buy it

AUK also ships a 14-day free trial with every feature unlocked and no payment details required. We would much rather you evaluated the app on the trial and bought it knowing it works for you than bought it blind and needed a refund. The refund guarantee exists as a backstop, not as the primary way to find out whether you like it.

3. How to request a refund

There are two routes, and either one works:

  1. Email us at skumar5190@gmail.com. Include the email address you used at checkout or your Paddle order reference so we can find the order. A one-line message is enough. We acknowledge requests within 2 business days and submit the refund to Paddle as soon as we have matched it to your order.
  2. Contact Paddle buyer support directly at paddle.net, the buyer-support portal run by Paddle as merchant of record for your order. You can look up your order there using your email address or order reference and raise the refund request without going through us at all.

4. How the refund is processed

Because Paddle.com is the merchant of record for every AUK order, Paddle processes the refund and issues it to your original payment method — the same card, PayPal account, or other method used at checkout. We cannot refund to a different card or account, and we cannot pay a refund out separately in cash or by transfer.

Once approved, the refund typically appears within 3–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider. Any sales tax, VAT, or GST collected on the original order is refunded along with it. Paddle emails you a confirmation when the refund is issued.

5. What happens to your licence

When a refund is issued, the associated licence key is deactivated and the licence permission to use the Software ends. Please remove AUK from your machines. Any workspaces, requests, and files you created remain your own plain files on your own disk — a refund does not touch, reclaim, or delete your data, because we never had it.

6. After 14 days

Outside the 14-day window we are not obliged to offer a refund, but we are reasonable people: if something has genuinely gone wrong — a duplicate charge, a purchase you did not recognise, a defect we could not fix, or a licence you were never able to activate — write to us and we will sort it out. Mandatory consumer rights under the law applicable to you are unaffected by this policy and continue to apply in addition to it.

7. Renewals

AUK is a one-time purchase, so there is no subscription to cancel and no recurring charge to be surprised by. If you later choose to buy an optional extension of your update window, that purchase is covered by the same 14-day guarantee.

8. Chargebacks

If you are unhappy, please contact us or Paddle first — a refund is nearly always quicker than a chargeback and costs you nothing. Raising a chargeback without contacting us results in the licence being deactivated while the dispute is investigated.

9. Contact

Refund requests and billing questions: skumar5190@gmail.com, or Paddle buyer support at paddle.net. This policy forms part of our Terms & Conditions.
