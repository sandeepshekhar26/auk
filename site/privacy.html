Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

This policy explains what data the AUK desktop application and the website at auk.deskmcp.com collect, and what happens to it. It is issued by the seller of AUK, identified by legal name in the Seller clause of our Terms & Conditions and in the footer of this page, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy.

The short version. AUK contains no telemetry, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Your workspaces, requests, responses, and secrets stay on your Mac. The app makes network calls that you did not initiate for exactly two reasons: to activate your licence, and to check GitHub for a newer release. This website is static and sets no cookies. We do not sell data, and there is nothing here to sell.

1. What the app does not collect

AUK does not include usage analytics, product telemetry, session recording, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, or any third-party SDK that reports back. We do not know which features you use, how often you open the app, or what your requests look like. This is a deliberate design decision, not a setting you have to find and switch off.

2. Your workspace data stays on your Mac

  • Workspaces, requests, environments, and scripts are stored as plain files on your own disk. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Responses and history — including any personal or confidential data returned by the APIs you call — stay local and are never transmitted to us.
  • Secrets such as API keys, tokens, and passwords are stored in the macOS keychain, managed by the operating system. We have no access to the keychain and no ability to read those values.
  • Git synchronisation is yours. If you sync a workspace with git, it goes to a remote you configure — your GitHub, GitLab, or self-hosted server. We operate no sync service and are not in that path. Whatever you push is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy.
  • The requests you send go directly from your Mac to the endpoints you type in. There is no proxy of ours in between.

3. Licence activation

When you activate a purchased licence, the app sends the licence key together with:

  • the email address associated with your purchase, so we can identify the licence and re-send your key if you lose it; and
  • a hashed machine identifier — a one-way hash derived from your device, used only to count how many of your three permitted machines are active and to let you deactivate one. We do not store the underlying device identifier, and the hash cannot be reversed to reveal your hardware, your name, or your location.

We use this data solely to issue, validate, and support your licence, and to enforce the three-machine limit set out in the Terms. It is retained for as long as the licence is valid plus the period required for tax and accounting records, then deleted. It is not used for marketing and is not shared with anyone except as required to provide the service or by law. Trial use does not require activation and does not send this data.

4. Update checks

The app periodically asks the public GitHub Releases API whether a newer version of AUK exists. The request contains no licence data, no account data, and no information about your workspaces. As with any HTTPS request, GitHub will see your IP address, the requested URL, and a user agent as part of serving it; that data is handled by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement, not by us. You can disable update checks in the app's settings.

5. This website

auk.deskmcp.com is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers, no advertising pixels, and no social widgets. There is no login and no form that collects personal data.

Two ordinary technical points: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's font servers (which will see your IP address and user agent) when a page loads; and the static host serving this site keeps standard server access logs containing IP addresses and requested URLs, retained briefly for security and operational purposes.

6. Payments

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com as the merchant of record. When you buy a licence, Paddle collects and processes the data required to complete the sale — including your name, email address, billing address, country, and payment card or other payment details — and handles fraud prevention and tax compliance.

We never see or store your card number or bank details. What we receive from Paddle is limited to the information needed to fulfil the order and support you: your email address, the product and licence purchased, the order reference, and your billing country for tax records.

Paddle's handling of your data is governed by the Paddle Privacy Policy.

7. Email correspondence

If you email us for support, a refund, or any other reason, we hold your message and email address for as long as needed to deal with it and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list and do not send marketing email without your explicit request.

8. Legal basis and sharing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of a contract (issuing and validating your licence, providing support) and on our legitimate interests in preventing licence abuse and keeping the software secure.

We share personal data only with the service providers needed to run this business — principally Paddle for payments and the provider that hosts this website — and only to the extent required, or where we are legally obliged to disclose it. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, delete it, restrict or object to our use of it, or provide it in a portable format. Email skumar5190@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Because we hold so little — essentially your email address, licence records, and any correspondence — such requests are usually straightforward.

Requests concerning your payment data should go to Paddle, which is the controller for that data as merchant of record. If you believe we have mishandled your data you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

AUK is a developer tool intended for professional use and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If our practices change we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes affecting how we handle personal data will be described here rather than made silently.
